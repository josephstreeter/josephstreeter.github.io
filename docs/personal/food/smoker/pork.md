## Stuffed Pork Tenderloin

Pork tenderloin, butterflied, filled with cream cheese, shredded cheese, and pinto or black beans, then rolled, wrapped in bacon, and smoked. Pork tenderloin is lean and small, so this is a short cook. Pull it on temperature, not on time, so it doesn't dry out.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Cooker** | Smoker |
| **Prep time** | 30 minutes, plus 2–4 hours to marinate (optional) |
| **Cook time** | 1½–2 hours |
| **Servings** | 4–6 |
| **Cook temp** | 250°F |
| **Target internal temp** | 145°F |
| **Wood** | Apple or cherry |

### Ingredients

**Pork:**

- 2 pork tenderloins (about 1–1¼ lb each), silver skin removed
- 8–10 slices thin-cut bacon (optional)
- Kitchen twine or toothpicks

**Rub:**

- Your favorite pork rub, or a mix of:
  - 2 tsp salt
  - 1 tsp black pepper
  - 1 tsp garlic powder
  - 1 tsp onion powder
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

**Marinade (optional):**

- About 1 cup maple syrup
- 1 gallon-size zip-top bag

**Stuffing:**

- 1 package (8 oz) cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup shredded cheese (cheddar or mozzarella)
- ¾ cup pinto or black beans (about half a 15-oz can), drained and rinsed

**Glaze:**

- 1 can (14 oz) jellied cranberry sauce
- Or barbecue sauce or a honey mustard glaze

### Instructions

1. **Marinate (optional).** Put both tenderloins in the zip-top bag, add the maple syrup, press out the air, and seal. Refrigerate for 2–4 hours or overnight. Drain before butterflying.
2. **Make the stuffing.** Mix the cream cheese, shredded cheese, and beans in a bowl. Set aside.
3. **Butterfly the tenderloins.** Cut each tenderloin lengthwise down the middle, stopping about ½ inch from the other side, and open it like a book. Cover it with plastic wrap and pound it to an even ½-inch thickness.
4. **Stuff and roll.** Spread half the stuffing over each tenderloin, leaving a ½-inch border around the edges. Roll each one up tightly, starting from a long side.
5. **Season.** Mix the rub ingredients and coat the outside of each roll.
6. **Wrap and tie.** If you're using bacon, lay the slices side by side and roll the tenderloin up in them. Tie with twine every 1½–2 inches, or secure with toothpicks.
7. **Preheat the smoker** to 250°F with apple or cherry wood.
8. **Smoke** seam side down until the internal temperature reaches 135°F in the thickest part of the meat (not the stuffing), about 1¼–1¾ hours.
9. **Glaze.** Warm the cranberry sauce in a small saucepan and whisk until smooth. Brush it over the top of each roll and continue smoking for the last 15–20 minutes.
10. **Finish.** Pull the pork at 145°F. For crispier bacon, sear it over high heat or on a hot grill for 1–2 minutes per side before pulling.
11. **Rest** for 10 minutes. Remove the twine and slice into 1-inch rounds. Serve any leftover cranberry glaze on the side.

### Notes

- If the bacon isn't crisp by the time the pork reaches temperature, raise the smoker to 325°F for the last 20–30 minutes, or sear it at the end.
- A probe thermometer is the best tool for this cook. The tenderloin goes from juicy to dry quickly above 150°F.
- **Alternate stuffing:** apple, sage, and breakfast sausage. Cook the sausage completely before stuffing, because the center of the roll won't reach a safe temperature for raw sausage.
